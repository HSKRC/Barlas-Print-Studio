FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Questions about printing in Melbourne?

What printing services does Barlas Print Studio offer?

We offer business cards, flyers, brochures, posters, stickers, labels, vinyl and window graphics, banners and signage. Contact us for custom requests.

How can I get a printing quote?

Use our online quote form to provide the product, quantity, dimensions and artwork. We will review your requirements and prepare a tailored quote.

Can I upload my own artwork?

Yes. You can upload artwork with your quote request. If your file needs print preparation, ask about design support.

Do you offer pickup or delivery?

Pickup and delivery options depend on the order and location. We confirm availability and any applicable charges when quoting.

How long will my printing order take?

Turnaround varies by product, quantity, materials and finishing. We confirm an estimated schedule before production.
