Privacy Policy

How customer information and artwork are handled.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, email, phone number, quote details, order details and artwork files when you use our website or request printing services.

How we use it

We use this information to prepare quotes, produce orders, communicate about jobs, provide account and order-tracking features, improve our services and meet reasonable business record-keeping requirements.

Artwork and files

Artwork uploaded through a signed-in account is stored in private storage and is intended only for job preparation, production and related customer support. Access is restricted by account permissions and administration controls.

Accounts and security

Customer account authentication is provided through Supabase. We use access controls and row-level security to limit access to customer and order information.

Third-party services

Our website may use service providers for hosting, authentication, storage, email, payments or analytics. Those providers process information according to their own terms and privacy practices.

When you search for a delivery address, the address text you type is sent to Photon, a geocoding service using OpenStreetMap data, to return address suggestions. The address you select is included with your quote request.

Retention

We retain business and order information for as long as reasonably needed for production, customer service, record keeping, dispute handling and legal obligations.

Cookies and local storage

We use essential browser storage to support customer sign-in, account security and website functionality. On pages with a cookie preference banner, you may accept or decline optional cookies. We do not activate optional analytics or marketing cookies through this banner unless those services are separately configured to respect your choice. You can change your choice by clearing this site’s local storage in your browser.

Your choices

You may contact us through the website to ask about personal information associated with your account or order.

Updates

This policy may be updated when our services, providers or business operations change.
